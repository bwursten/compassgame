How to play

The goal: walk three legs of 100 feet, turning 120° each time, and finish standing next to the stick you started at.

  1. Pick a bearing for leg 1 — any number you like — and turn the dial to it.
  2. Turn yourself until the red needle sits inside the red shed. The shed lights up when you're lined up.
  3. Press Walk 100 feet. Footprints appear as you go, one for every 10 feet.
  4. Add 120° to your last bearing and set the dial again. If the total goes over 360°, subtract 360.
  5. Line up and walk again. After the third leg, see how close you came to your stick.

Ways to turn things

  • Twist the numbered dial to set your bearing.
  • Twist the clear baseplate around it to turn yourself.
  • Swipe across the field to turn, or tap a spot to face it.
  • Or use the arrow buttons under each step.

The needle always points north — it never moves. The compass turns around it, exactly as it would in your hands.
