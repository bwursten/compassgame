How to play the Compass Game

The goal: walk three legs of 100 feet and end up back at the stick where you started.

Every leg is three steps

  1. Set dial. Turn the numbered ring to your bearing, then press Next.
  2. Turn. Turn yourself until the red needle sits inside the red shed.
  3. Walk. The big button turns green. Press it.

Which bearing?

  • Leg 1: any bearing you like.
  • Leg 2: add or subtract 120°. Either one works.
  • Leg 3: keep going the same way: add again, or subtract again.

Over 360°? Subtract 360. Below 0°? Add 360.

Ways to turn

  • Twist the numbered ring to set the dial.
  • Twist the clear baseplate, or swipe the map, to turn yourself.
  • Or use the arrow buttons.
  • Press 3D view on the map to see the field in 3D. North is still at the top.

Levels, stars and badges

Beginner tells you the next bearing and which way to turn. Change level with the gear button. Land within 10 feet for 3 stars. The medal button shows your badges.
